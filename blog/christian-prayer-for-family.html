Christian Prayer for Family: Simple Prayers for Your Home

Quick Answer

Christian prayer for family can cover ordinary life as well as major concerns. Pray for each family member by name and bring their relationships, decisions, work, school, health, and spiritual growth before God.

A Simple Approach

For children, pray for wisdom, protection, healthy friendships, character, and growing faith. For marriage, pray for patience, communication, forgiveness, wisdom, and mutual care. Short prayers at meals, bedtime, or before school can become natural family habits.

Prayer You Can Use

Keep a family prayer list and revisit it weekly. Record answered prayers as well as continuing requests so your family has a simple record of what you have brought to God.
