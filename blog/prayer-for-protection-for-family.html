Prayer for Protection for Yourself and Your Family

A practical Christian prayer guide for asking God for protection, wisdom, peace, and care for yourself and the people you love.

What does a prayer for protection include?

A prayer for protection can ask for God's care over a person, family, home, travel, work, relationships, and daily decisions. It can also ask for wisdom to recognize risks and respond responsibly rather than treating prayer as a substitute for practical safety.

Pray specifically for family members

Instead of one general request, name the people and areas of life that concern you. You might pray for a child's school day, a spouse's work, a parent's health, safe travel, or wisdom during a difficult decision. Specific prayer can make a family prayer time more focused.

Include wisdom and peace

Protection prayers can include requests for discernment, patience, courage, and peace. These themes are useful when the situation involves uncertainty because they address how you respond as well as what you hope will happen.

Use Scripture in your prayer time

Read a short passage about God's care, refuge, wisdom, or presence before writing your prayer. You can then respond to the passage in your own words. A prayer journal makes it easy to save passages that you want to revisit.

Create a repeatable family practice

A short evening prayer, a morning prayer before school, or a weekly family prayer time can make protection prayer part of a broader spiritual routine. Keep it simple enough that everyone can participate.
