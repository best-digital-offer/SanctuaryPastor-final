Prayer for Strength: What to Pray When You Feel Tired

Quick answer: A prayer for strength can be simple and honest. When you feel physically, emotionally, or spiritually tired, you can tell God exactly where you are and ask for the strength needed for today.

A practical starting point

You do not have to pretend everything is fine. Christian prayer can include weariness, uncertainty, disappointment, and the need for help. Begin by naming the situation clearly.

Build a simple routine

Ask for strength for the next step rather than trying to solve the entire future in one prayer. You might ask for patience during a difficult conversation, courage for a needed decision, endurance through a demanding season, or wisdom about what to do next.

Keep it realistic

Isaiah 40:31 is a familiar passage for Christian reflection on renewed strength. Psalm 46:1 also describes God as a refuge and strength. Reading a short passage before praying can give your prayer a biblical starting point.

A prayer you can use

Try this simple prayer: Lord, I am tired and I need Your strength. Give me wisdom for what is in front of me, courage for what I need to face, and grace to rest when I need to rest. Help me trust You today. Amen.

Make it part of daily life

Keep your prayer practice realistic. A five-minute prayer followed by a short Scripture reading can be meaningful. You can also write one sentence about what you need and return to it later.
