Prayer for Guidance: How to Pray When You Need Direction

Quick Answer

A prayer for guidance brings decisions and uncertainty before God while you continue to gather information and seek wise counsel. Start by describing the decision clearly and identifying what you know and do not know.

A Simple Approach

James 1:5 is commonly used in Christian reflection on asking God for wisdom. Read a short Scripture passage, ask for clarity, and give yourself permission to pause when an immediate answer is not necessary.

Prayer You Can Use

A simple prayer is: God, I need wisdom. Help me see this situation clearly, understand what matters, and make a decision consistent with truth, love, and responsibility. Give me patience where I need to wait and courage where I need to act. Amen.
