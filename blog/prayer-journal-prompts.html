25 Prayer Journal Prompts for a Deeper Prayer Life

Quick answer: Prayer journal prompts can make it easier to begin when you do not know what to write. They are not formulas; they are starting points that help you slow down and pray honestly.

A practical starting point

Try these prompts: What am I thankful for today? What concern do I need to surrender? Where do I need wisdom? Who needs prayer today? What Scripture encouraged me recently? What decision needs guidance? Where am I learning patience? Where have I seen provision? What relationship needs prayer? What do I need courage to do? What do I need to forgive? What habit do I want to place before God? What am I worried about? What can I release today? What truth from Scripture do I want to remember? What answered prayer can I record? What am I asking God to help me understand? Where do I need rest? What is one faithful step I can take today? What can I praise God for? What challenge should I pray through before reacting? Who can I encourage? What lesson from a difficult season should I remember? What hope do I want to carry into tomorrow? What would an honest prayer sound like right now?

Build a simple routine

You can use one prompt each day or choose several when you have more time. A short entry is enough. Consistency matters more than writing a long page.

Keep it realistic

It can also help to revisit older entries. Seeing previous requests, reflections, and answered prayers can help you recognize patterns and remember how your perspective changed over time.

A prayer you can use

For a structured routine, combine a prompt with a short Scripture passage and finish with prayer. This turns journaling into a connected practice rather than simply recording thoughts.
