Night Prayer Before Sleep: A Simple Christian Routine

Quick Answer

A night prayer can be a quiet way to close the day. It does not need to be long. Begin with gratitude, reflect honestly on the day, pray for people you care about, and place tomorrow in God's hands.

A Simple Approach

Try this simple pattern: thank God for three things, confess anything that needs attention, pray for family and friends, and ask for wisdom and peace for tomorrow. You can write one prayer request in a journal before going to sleep.

Prayer You Can Use

A simple prayer is: Father, thank You for carrying me through this day. Forgive me where I have fallen short and help me learn from today. I place tomorrow in Your hands. Give me peaceful rest and wisdom for the new day. Amen.
