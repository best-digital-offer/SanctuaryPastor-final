Morning Prayer for a New Day: A Simple 10-Minute Routine

Quick Answer

A morning prayer can help you begin the day intentionally instead of immediately reacting to notifications and tasks. Ten focused minutes can be enough.

A Simple Approach

Minutes 1–2: become quiet. Minutes 3–5: read a short Scripture passage. Minutes 6–7: thank God for several things. Minutes 8–9: surrender your schedule and concerns. Minute 10: pray for someone else and choose one practical intention for the day.

Prayer You Can Use

A short prayer could be: Father, thank You for this new day. Guide my thoughts, words, decisions, and actions. Give me wisdom for my responsibilities, patience with people, courage for difficult moments, and a heart that remembers You throughout the day. Amen.
